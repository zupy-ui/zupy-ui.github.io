Why zupy

Feels good. Works properly.

The fun is on the surface. Underneath it's the boring, solid stuff you'd want anyway.

Made to be pressed

Hard shadows and springy motion. Raised things sink into their shadow when you press them.

Accessible underneath

Built on Base UI, so keyboard, focus and screen reader behavior come for free.

Themes from tokens

Color, shape and depth live in one set of tokens. Swap a theme and everything follows.

Fits your stack

Plain React and Tailwind v4 classes. Typed props, a render prop for any element, no lock-in.

Icons that match

A tactile icon set drawn with the same outlines and shadows, plus a helper to make your own.

Docs your AI can read

Every page has a Markdown copy, and llms.txt points your assistant at all of them.
